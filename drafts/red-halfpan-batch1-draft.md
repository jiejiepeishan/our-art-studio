### 1. Pinax · Pyrrol Scarlet  
`pinax-pr255-pyrrol-scarlet` · PR255 · #E84530 · half-pan · staining · ◈

**temp_role**  
`Warm clean scarlet · Pyrrol (PR255) · fire-engine primary · staining · ◈`

**ace_note**  
Fire-engine clean scarlet — modern pyrrol punch without muddy undertones.

**PR255** is late-century organic fire: high chroma, clean warm scarlet, stains with purpose. Florals, warning accents, design punch without earth dirt. Same molecule family as Schmincke **Vermilion** half-pan — manners contest, one seat.

**Dual advice:** one PR255 scarlet (Pinax *or* Vermilion). Vs Cad Red Light: pyrrol often cleaner/stainier; cad is butter-opaque cover. Vs Geranium (PR253): both modern scarlets — swatch, don’t stack three fires.

**ace_history**  
Pyrrol reds (PR255 family) are late-century organics prized for lightfast fire — the modern answer to mercury vermilion romance without the poison.

### 2. Schmincke · Vermilion  
`sch-hp-365-vermilion` · PR255 · #E34234 · half-pan · more body

**temp_role**  
`Warm bright scarlet · PR255 “vermilion” · travel-tin heat · more body`

**ace_note**  
PR255 vermilion half-pan — bright warm scarlet, distinct from earthy English Venetian red beside it in the travel tin.

Name says vermilion; chemistry is **modern PR255**, not cinnabar. Bright warm scarlet that sits next to English Red as *heat vs brick* — the original note’s travel-tin lesson is the whole dual. More body than a pure stain wash; still not cadmium’s heavy cover.

**Dual advice:** same PR255 seat as Pinax Pyrrol Scarlet. Keep one. English Red is the earth opposite, not a twin.

**ace_history**  
Vermilion once meant mercury sulfide; Horadam keeps the name on lightfast pyrrole chemistry. Creamy rewet for pocket tins.

### 3. Schmincke · Geranium Red  
`sch-hp-350-geranium-red` · PR253 · #E03A4A · half-pan · staining

**temp_role**  
`Warm floral scarlet · Pyrrole geranium (PR253) · garden accent · staining`

**ace_note**  
Bright geranium red — garden flowers and bold accents.

**PR253** pyrrole scarlet aimed at garden florals and illustration pop — permanent clean red with a slight pink lean vs pure fire-engine PR255. Staining; great petals, loud in shadows if overdone.

**Dual advice:** modern warm scarlet seat with PR255 — one high-chroma warm red max in a tiny tin unless you paint only flowers. Vs Cad Light: geranium is cleaner stain; cad is opaque classic.

**ace_history**  
Geranium red (PR253) is modern pyrrole floral scarlet — permanent pop for illustrators. Horadam creamy rewet.

### 4. Schmincke · Cadmium Red Light  
`sch-hp-cadmium-red-light` · PR108 · #E04E3A · half-pan · more opaque

**temp_role**  
`Warm opaque red · Cadmium (PR108) · florals, fruit, lips · cover & correct`

**ace_note**  
Classic warm red. Bold florals, ripe fruit, lips on portraits. Opaque enough to correct mistakes.

Cadmium is the **heavy warm red**: opaque, assertive, Salon heat. Rescues washed-out petals; muddies delicate glazes if you treat it like quin. Mix *small* into flesh and earth; solo for fruit and lips.

**Dual advice:** one opaque warm red. If pyrrol/geranium already give clean scarlet, Cad is optional muscle. Studio respect, not panic.

**ace_history**  
Cadmium red (PR108) replaced fugitive vermilion in commercial and fine art — opaque warmth for florals, flesh accents, and corrections.

### 5. Schmincke · English Venetian Red  
`sch-hp-english-red` · PR101 · #A84B3A · half-pan · more opaque

**temp_role**  
`Warm earth red · Iron oxide (PR101) · terracotta / brick · not vermilion`

**ace_note**  
Earthy PR101 Venetian red — terracotta brick warmth, not vermilion heat. Opaque and color-intense.

Iron oxide brick: architecture, tile, quiet warm structure. Won’t give clean scarlet florals; will give walls and earth that feel solid. Cousin to transparent iron oxides in earth/brown families — here the **opaque English/Venetian** cut.

**Dual advice:** earth-red seat — not a second Cad or pyrrol. One brick red. Vs Madder Red Dark: earth opaque vs transparent wine-rose.

**ace_history**  
English Venetian red (PR101) is calcined iron oxide — brick-and-terracotta tone British watercolourists used beside raw sienna on muddy Thames sketches.

### 6. Schmincke · Madder Red Dark  
`sch-hp-354-madder-red-dark` · PR209 · #8B2A3A · half-pan · staining · transparent

**temp_role**  
`Cool-warm deep rose-red · Quin (PR209) · wine-stain glaze · transparent`

**ace_note**  
Deep madder rose — wine-stain florals and old-master shadows.

**PR209** transparent quin red-rose — deep wine stain for florals and glaze shadows, not brick cover. “Madder” is mood; permanence is modern organic. Bridges toward cool carmine/ruby in batch 2 without being PV19.

**Dual advice:** transparent deep rose-red seat. Vs English Red: glow vs brick. Vs batch 2 Permanent Carmine / Ruby (PV19): different codes — swatch if you only keep one dark rose-red.

**ace_history**  
Quinacridone reds (PR209) are transparent organics built for glow — modern glazing reds that keep florals luminous after true madder’s fade.
